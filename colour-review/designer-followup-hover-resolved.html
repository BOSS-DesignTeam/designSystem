The problem

The orange highlight #fa9016 is also the app’s general hover colour on links, breadcrumbs, sortable headers, tabs and clickable icons: 79 rules. Under Q1 the warning text role darkens to #a85800, so every one of them would turn dark brown on hover. The spinner steam and two trend arrows had the same problem.

QuestionAnswerWhy
I1 Hover on 79 rulesBRAND, with 5 exceptionsHover is an interaction state, not a warning, so it leaves the warning role (H1: orange = warnings only). Brand matches active and breadcrumb (H1, Q3).
I2 Loader steamICON-WARNINGDecorative. Keeps today’s orange with no visible change. Remove when the bowl loader is replaced (BOPD-1166).
I3 Trend arrowsICON-WARNINGThey are glyphs, so under H2 they stay bright orange. Trend direction is a real status signal.
